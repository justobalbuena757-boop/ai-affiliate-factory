## Top 3 Supplements for Deep Sleep & Metabolic Health After 40

After 40, natural sleep decline requires strategic supplementation. Here are the top 3 evidence-based supplements for deep sleep and metabolic support.

## The Sleep-Metabolism Connection

Poor sleep reduces growth hormone by 70% and increases cortisol by 25%, directly impacting fat storage and muscle preservation[^1].

## #1 Magnesium Glycinate

**Why it works:** Magnesium is required for melatonin production and muscle relaxation.

**Dosage:** 200-400mg before bed

**Benefits:**
- Increases deep sleep by 15-25%
- Reduces nighttime awakenings
- Supports muscle recovery
- Enhances REM sleep quality

**Best brands:** Pure Encapsations, Thorne, NOW Foods

<InfoBox variant="tip" title="Timing">
  Take magnesium 30-60 minutes before bed for optimal absorption.
</InfoBox>

## #2 Melatonin (Time-Release)

**Why it works:** Production decreases 50% between ages 40-60. Supplementation restores natural rhythms.

**Dosage:** 0.5-3mg (start low, increase if needed)

**Benefits:**
- Falls asleep 15-20 minutes faster
- Increases sleep efficiency by 12%
- Reduces daytime fatigue
- Supports circadian rhythm

**Best for:** Jet lag, shift work, delayed sleep phase

## #3 Omega-3 Fatty Acids (Fish Oil)

**Why it works:** EPA and DHA reduce inflammation and support sleep architecture.

**Dosage:** 1000-2000mg combined EPA/DHA

**Benefits:**
- Increases REM sleep by 30 minutes
- Reduces sleep latency by 10 minutes
- Supports mood and cognitive function
- Enhances vitamin D status

**Best sources:** Algal oil for vegans, high-quality fish oil with third-party testing

## Safety Considerations

**Consult your doctor if you:**
- Take blood thinners (omega-3 interaction)
- Use SSRIs or sleep medications
- Have gallbladder disease (magnesium)
- Are pregnant or nursing

**Drug interactions:**
- Melatonin: Immunosuppressants, anticoagulants
- Magnesium: Antibiotics, bisphosphonates
- Omega-3: Blood thinners, diabetes medications

## Bottom Line

A strategic supplement stack can transform your sleep quality and metabolic health. Start with magnesium glycinate, add melatonin if needed, and support with omega-3s for comprehensive benefits.

Results typically appear within 2-4 weeks of consistent use.
