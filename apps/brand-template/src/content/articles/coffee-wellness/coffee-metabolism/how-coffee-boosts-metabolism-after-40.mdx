## How Coffee Boosts Metabolism

### Thermogenesis: The Heat Effect

When you consume caffeine, your body increases its heat production (thermogenesis). This process burns additional calories as the body works harder to maintain temperature.

- **Energy expenditure increase**: 10-11% from thermogenesis alone
- **Fat oxidation increase**: Up to 29% higher in adults over 40
- **Time to peak effect**: 30-60 minutes after consumption

### The Role of Adenosine Receptors

Coffee blocks adenosine receptors, which:
1. Reduces feelings of fatigue
2. Increases alertness and focus
3. Triggers the release of adrenaline
4. Activates fat-burning enzymes

<InfoBox variant="tip" title="Timing Matters">
  Consume coffee 30-60 minutes before your most demanding activity for maximum metabolic benefit.
</InfoBox>

## Practical Implementation

### Morning Protocol
1. **Wait 60-90 minutes after waking** (cortisol is naturally high)
2. **Consume 2-3 cups** of black coffee or cold brew
3. **Add MCT oil or grass-fed butter** for sustained energy
4. **Hydrate**: Coffee is diuretic; match with water

### Best Practices
- Avoid adding sugar (spikes insulin)
- Choose high-quality, organic beans
- Consider cold brew for lower acidity
- Cycle off caffeine 1 day per week
